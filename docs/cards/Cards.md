# Knowledge Card Index

This index pulls the concepts scattered across the chapters into cards, organized as a web along the four layers of the [layered architecture](/layers/Layers) plus two cross-cutting tracks. Each card carries a one-sentence definition, its neighboring cards (interlinked to the corresponding deep-dive pages), and a source page. For a flat lookup table of individual terms, see the [glossary](/cards/Glossary); to read the lineage by year, see the [development timeline](/timeline) and the [consensus algorithm timeline](/consensus-timeline); for the consolidated view of concepts and papers, see the [knowledge compendium](/knowledge).

## How to read it

- Cards connect through "adjacent" links: each card links to three to five others, so finishing one layer's cards lets you slide naturally into an entry point of a neighboring layer — this is a web, not a table of contents.
- "Source" points to the site's deep-dive pages; the original citations for papers and whitepapers live in the "four elements" block at the top of each deep-dive page.
- The two cross-cutting tracks span layers: **ledger and transactions** determine how state is recorded (the interface between the settlement and storage layers), while **attack and defense** determines which layer gets broken first (when reading case studies, locate the layer before reading the attack).

## Settlement layer: which states are irreversible

- **Finality**: the promise that a state is irreversible, in two flavors — probabilistic (more confirmations make reorgs harder) and deterministic (irreversible once votes reach a threshold) | Adjacent: [longest chain and fork choice](/consensus/GHOST), [BFT voting](/consensus/PBFT), [weak subjectivity](/consensus/POS) | Deep dive: [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement)
- **Longest chain and fork choice**: pick the head by cumulative weight; GHOST counts forked blocks into the weight, which is what makes shorter block intervals safe | Adjacent: [probabilistic finality](/consensus/POW), [LMD-GHOST](/consensus/Gasper), [selfish mining](/hacking/SelfishMiningAttack), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [GHOST](/consensus/GHOST)
- **BFT voting**: tolerating f Byzantine nodes requires n ≥ 3f+1; agreement by 2/3 is final | Adjacent: [QC](/consensus/HotStuff), [three-chain commit](/consensus/DiemBFT), [view change](/consensus/PBFT), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [PBFT](/consensus/PBFT)
- **QC (quorum certificate)**: aggregate 2f+1 signatures into a single verifiable object, which makes voting pipelineable | Adjacent: [HotStuff pipelining](/consensus/HotStuff), [MaxBFT](/consensus/MaxBFT), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [HotStuff](/consensus/HotStuff)
- **Weak subjectivity**: new PoS nodes must sync from a recent trusted state; the chain alone cannot tell true from false | Adjacent: [long-range attack](/hacking/LongRangeAttack), [checkpoints](/consensus/Tendermint), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [PoS](/consensus/POS)
- **Slashing**: double signing and surround voting are punished with verifiable evidence — the economic core of PoS security | Adjacent: [Nothing-at-Stake](/consensus/POS), [inactivity leak](/consensus/Gasper), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [Gasper](/consensus/Gasper)
- **Finality gadget**: block production and finalization split into two independent components, each upgradable on its own | Adjacent: [BABE](/consensus/BABE), [Casper FFG](/consensus/Gasper), [GRANDPA prefix voting](/consensus/GRANDPA), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [GRANDPA](/consensus/GRANDPA)
- **UNL and quorum slices**: a trust list (or self-chosen slices) decides who is eligible to vote; a misconfiguration forks the network | Adjacent: [RPCA](/consensus/RPCA), [FBA](/consensus/FBA), [Sybil attack](/hacking/SybilAttack), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [FBA](/consensus/FBA)
- **Random sortition**: VRFs keep block-proposal rights unpredictable until revelation, leaving targeted attacks nothing to aim at | Adjacent: [VDF random clock](/consensus/POT), [Algorand secret sortition](/consensus/Algorand), [BABE blind assignment](/consensus/BABE), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [Ouroboros](/consensus/Ouroboros)
- **DAG consensus**: block production and ordering proceed asynchronously over a directed acyclic graph, with no fixed block interval | Adjacent: [gossip-about-gossip voting](/consensus/Hashgraph), [DAG mempool](/consensus/NarwhalBullshark), [super checkpoints](/consensus/Lachesis), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [Lachesis](/consensus/Lachesis)
- **Paxos and majorities**: any two majorities intersect ⇒ a decided value cannot be overturned — the entire skeleton of CFT consensus | Adjacent: [RAFT](/consensus/RAFT), [BFT voting](/consensus/PBFT), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [Paxos](/consensus/Paxos)
- **uncertified DAG**: skip per-round certificates; a "three-round direct-parent-chain" commit rule saves one network round trip | Adjacent: [DAG consensus](/consensus/Lachesis), [Narwhal mempool](/consensus/NarwhalBullshark), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [Mysticeti](/consensus/Mysticeti)
- **Tangle and the Coordinator**: a tip-approval DAG ledger fails to converge at low traffic, so finality degrades to the single point of the Coordinator | Adjacent: [DAG ledger](/model/DAG), [probabilistic finality](/consensus/POW), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [IOTA](/consensus/IOTA)
- **Shared security budget**: restaking rents the same slashing deterrent to multiple protocols; efficiency and correlated failure share a single root | Adjacent: [slashing](/consensus/Gasper), [finality](/layers/Settlement), [long-range attack](/hacking/LongRangeAttack) | Deep dive: [Restaking](/consensus/Restaking)
- **Random access to history**: block production must answer "do you still hold the old data at this random location?" — storage turns from a capacity race into a retrievability race | Adjacent: [PoST sustained holding](/consensus/PoST), [storage layer](/layers/Storage), [finality](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [SPoRA](/consensus/SPORA)
- **Physically falsifiable coverage claims**: third-party probing plus checks against physical law (inverse-square falloff, the speed-of-light bound) make "I have coverage here" impossible to fabricate | Adjacent: [Sybil resistance](/consensus/POW), [HoneyBadgerBFT](/consensus/HoneyBadgerBFT), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [Proof of Coverage](/consensus/ProofOfCoverage)
- **Two-tier BFT**: a small committee votes to preserve throughput while community checkpoint signatures provide the floor — splitting efficiency and decentralization across tiers, half each | Adjacent: [finality gadget](/consensus/GRANDPA), [BFT voting](/consensus/PBFT), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [Multi-Level BFT](/consensus/MultiLevelBFT)
- **Subjective-utility consensus**: when service quality cannot be formally verified, stake-weighted median trimming compresses "how everyone rates it" into settleable weights | Adjacent: [probabilistic validation (Livepeer)](/consensus/DPOS), [Sybil attack](/hacking/SybilAttack), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [Yuma](/consensus/Yuma)
- **BlockDAG consensus**: concurrent blocks all enter the graph and are then linearized; throughput scales with concurrency instead of a single serial chain | Adjacent: [GHOST fork choice](/consensus/GHOST), [DAG ledger](/model/DAG), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [GHOSTDAG](/consensus/GHOSTDAG)
- **Sharding**: split validation and state across multiple parallel subnets; throughput grows roughly linearly with the shard count | Adjacent: [PBFT voting](/consensus/PBFT), [parachains](/chains/Polkadot), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [sharding](/consensus/Sharding)
- **Proof of bandwidth**: prove "bandwidth was genuinely contributed," with verification happening in an offchain service network rather than block-producing consensus | Adjacent: [PoSpace](/consensus/PoSpace), [layered architecture](/layers/Layers), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [Proof of Bandwidth](/consensus/BandwidthProof)
- **NPoS**: nominators lend their stake to candidate validators, and the Phragmén algorithm evens the stake across validators | Adjacent: [BABE blind assignment](/consensus/BABE), [Polkadot](/chains/Polkadot), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [NPoS](/consensus/NPoS)
- **Proof of space and time**: PoSpace uses disk drives for sortition while PoT uses VDF serial time to keep the block cadence — two consensuses working in tandem | Adjacent: [PoT proof of time](/consensus/POT), [Chia](/chains/Chia), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [PoSpace](/consensus/PoSpace)
- **Consortium chains**: permissioned admission turns "who may keep the books" into a governance question, trading identity for deterministic finality and high throughput | Adjacent: [PoA](/consensus/POA), [Fabric channels](/consortium/Fabric), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [consortium chains](/consortium/Consortium)

## Compute layer: same input, same output

- **Deterministic execution**: no floating point, no randomness, no system calls — replay consistency is the precondition of consensus | Adjacent: [gas metering](/layers/Compute), [reentrancy](/hacking/SmartContractVulnerabilities) | Deep dive: [compute layer](/layers/Compute)
- **Gas metering**: turns "infinite loop" into a finite sequence of paid steps — the engineering answer to the halting problem | Adjacent: [deterministic execution](/layers/Compute), [dusting attack](/hacking/DustingAttack) | Deep dive: [compute layer](/layers/Compute)
- **Reentrancy**: an external call re-enters before the state update completes — the lesson of The DAO, 2016 | Adjacent: [smart contract vulnerabilities](/hacking/SmartContractVulnerabilities), [resource type system](/layers/Compute) | Deep dive: [compute layer](/layers/Compute)
- **Oracles**: the external facts a contract reads are not guaranteed by consensus; manipulation happens at the data-source layer | Adjacent: [flash loans](/hacking/SmartContractVulnerabilities), [settlement-layer trust anchor](/layers/Settlement) | Deep dive: [compute layer](/layers/Compute)
- **MEV**: ordering power gets extracted by searchers; front-running and sandwiching are structural risks | Adjacent: [fair ordering](/consensus/Hashgraph), [Sybil attack](/hacking/SybilAttack) | Deep dive: [compute layer](/layers/Compute)
- **Resource type system**: Move models assets as linear resources; the type system prevents copying and reentrancy | Adjacent: [reentrancy](/hacking/SmartContractVulnerabilities), [EVM compatibility](/chains/BinanceSmartChain) | Deep dive: [compute layer](/layers/Compute)
- **Contract upgrades and governance**: proxy patterns, timelocks, multisigs — the "maintainable surface" is the attack surface | Adjacent: [governance attacks](/consensus/DPOS), [bridge attacks](/hacking/CrossChainBridgeAttack) | Deep dive: [compute layer](/layers/Compute)
- **Ordering and soft confirmations**: the sequencer delivers experience first, L1 forced inclusion backstops censorship resistance, fraud or validity proofs settle the final word | Adjacent: [deterministic execution](/layers/Compute), [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement), [oracles](/layers/Compute) | Deep dive: [ordering and settlement](/consensus/Sequencing)
- **The four DApp layers**: frontend → wallet → contracts → indexer; state lives onchain while queries go through offchain indexes | Adjacent: [deterministic execution](/layers/Compute), [naming system](/layers/ServiceDiscovery), [gas metering](/layers/Compute) | Deep dive: [DApp architecture](/dapp/Dapp)

## Cross-cutting track B: attack and defense

- **51% reorgs**: with majority hashrate you can rewrite history; the boundary of the attack is its economic cost | Adjacent: [longest-chain rule](/consensus/GHOST), [probabilistic finality](/consensus/POW) | Deep dive: [large-hashrate attacks](/hacking/51Attack)
- **Long-range attacks**: old keys rebuild an alternative chain from genesis — the origin of PoS weak subjectivity | Adjacent: [weak subjectivity](/consensus/POS), [checkpoints](/consensus/Gasper) | Deep dive: [long-range attack](/hacking/LongRangeAttack)
- **Sybil attacks**: mass-mint identities to break "one person, one vote"; votes must be weighted by a resource | Adjacent: [Sybil resistance](/consensus/POW), [UNL](/consensus/RPCA) | Deep dive: [Sybil attack](/hacking/SybilAttack)
- **Double spends**: spend the same money twice; confirmation counts and finality are both its defenses | Adjacent: [finality](/layers/Settlement), [Finney attack](/hacking/FinneyAttack) | Deep dive: [double-spending attack](/hacking/DoubleSpendAttack)
- **Cross-chain bridge attacks**: signature-verification or permission flaws in trust-transfer channels — the costliest category | Adjacent: [finality gadget](/consensus/GRANDPA), [contract upgrades](/layers/Compute) | Deep dive: [cross-chain bridge attack](/hacking/CrossChainBridgeAttack)

## Three threads for reading the web

1. **Resources for finality**: hashrate ([PoW](/consensus/POW)) → stake ([PoS](/consensus/POS)) → identity ([PoA](/consensus/POA)) → disk ([PoSpace](/consensus/PoSpace)) → time ([PoT](/consensus/POT)) — each generation of consensus swaps the bookkeeping resource, not the voting method.
2. **Forks and ordering**: longest chain ([Bitcoin](/chains/Bitcoin)) → GHOST ([Ethereum's PoW era](/consensus/GHOST)) → vote-weighted ([LMD-GHOST](/consensus/Gasper)) → DAG ordering ([Narwhal/Bullshark](/consensus/NarwhalBullshark)) — ordering moves from chains to graphs.
3. **Onchain commitments and offchain retrieval**: state roots ([Merkle tree](/algo/MerkleTree)) → DA sampling ([storage layer](/layers/Storage)) → proofs of storage ([PoST](/consensus/PoST)) → content addressing ([storage layer](/layers/Storage)) — "on the chain" and "retrievable" are two different layers' business.
