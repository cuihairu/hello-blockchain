# Well-Known Blockchain Projects

Here are some relatively well-known blockchain projects and their consensus algorithms:

1. **Bitcoin**:
   - **Consensus algorithm**: Proof of Work (PoW)

2. **Ethereum**:
   - **Consensus algorithm**: previously Proof of Work (PoW); as of 2022 it formally moved to Proof of Stake (PoS) through The Merge, with consensus driven by the Beacon Chain.

3. **Polkadot**:
   - **Consensus algorithm**: Nominated Proof of Stake (NPoS)

4. **Cardano**:
   - **Consensus algorithm**: Ouroboros Proof of Stake (Ouroboros PoS)

5. **Tether**:
   - **Note**: USDT is a stablecoin issued on multiple blockchains (initially based on Bitcoin's Omni Layer, later extended to Ethereum ERC-20, TRON TRC-20, and others); it is not an independent public chain, and its security is inherited from the consensus mechanism of the underlying chain.

6. **Litecoin**:
   - **Consensus algorithm**: Proof of Work (PoW), the same as Bitcoin, using the Scrypt algorithm.

7. **Bytom**:
   - **Consensus algorithm**: Tensority Proof of Work (PoW), a special design used to enable asset exchange and distribution.

8. **Cosmos**:
   - **Consensus algorithm**: Tendermint Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT)

9. **EOS.IO (EOS)**:
   - **Consensus algorithm**: Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)

10. **Stellar**:
    - **Consensus algorithm**: Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP)

11. **Solana**:
    - **Consensus algorithm**: Proof of History (PoH), a verifiable clock, plus Tower BFT (PoS-based)

12. **BSC, Binance Smart Chain**:
    - **Consensus algorithm**: Proof of Staked Authority (PoSA, Parlia), with 21 validators taking turns producing blocks

13. **TRON**:
    - **Consensus algorithm**: Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS), with 27 super representatives taking turns producing blocks; see [TRON](/chains/TRON)

14. **TON (The Open Network)**:
    - **Consensus algorithm**: Catchain consensus (BFT) plus PoS staking to elect validators, with a mainchain-plus-workchain sharded architecture; see [TON](/chains/TON)

The consensus algorithms across these projects are all different, and each algorithm has its own strengths and characteristics in terms of security, scalability, and decentralization.
