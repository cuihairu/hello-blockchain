# BSC Binance Smart Chain

Binance Smart Chain (BSC) is a smart contract platform and blockchain network launched by Binance, the world's leading cryptocurrency exchange. It aims to give developers a fast, low-cost environment for building decentralized applications (DApps), and through its integration with Binance Chain it provides cross-chain compatibility and asset transfer capability. Here is a detailed introduction to Binance Smart Chain:


## Whitepaper and Specifications

No formal whitepaper — the Binance official documentation and the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/bnb-chain/bsc) are authoritative; mainnet launched on 2020-09-01, and it was renamed BNB Chain in 2022.

## What Problem It Solves

Ethereum congestion and high gas fees kept ordinary users out — an exchange ecosystem wanted a chain with "an EVM-equivalent experience, fast blocks, and low fees."

## Technical Contributions

- **PoSA consensus**: 21 validators take turns producing blocks (the Parlia engine, a Geth fork), with 3-second blocks — compare the mechanics with [DPoS](/consensus/DPOS) and [PoA](/consensus/POA).
- **Full EVM compatibility**: Ethereum applications migrate over unchanged, setting the precedent for "compatible chains quickly cloning an ecosystem" (later followed by TRON's TVM, OKC, and others).
- **Fusion with the Beacon Chain (2024)**: governance, staking, and native fast finality consolidated into a single chain.

## Limitations and Public Incidents

- Validators are concentrated in exchange-affiliated entities, so decentralization and censorship resistance are weak (contrasted with the [public-chain](/chains/Bitcoin) ideal).
- Public incidents: on 2021-12-01 the whole network halted for about two and a half hours (an IAVL upgrade defect); on 2021-10-01 the cross-chain bridge was drained of 570 million USD (a bridge contract defect, not the chain itself); and 2023-10 saw another brief block-production pause.

## Additional Notes
### Basic Features of Binance Smart Chain

1. **Dual-chain architecture**:
   - Binance Smart Chain uses a dual-chain architecture, running in parallel with Binance Chain. Binance Chain focuses on high-speed transaction processing and on-chain exchange functionality, while BSC focuses on smart contracts and DApp development.

2. **Consensus mechanism**:
   - Binance Smart Chain uses PoSA (Proof of Staked Authority; the implementation is called Parlia) consensus: about 21 validators, elected through BNB staking and governance, take turns producing blocks; it combines the traits of proof-of-stake and authorized authority nodes, with a block time of about 3 seconds.

3. **EVM compatibility**:
   - BSC is compatible with the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM), which means developers can use Ethereum's smart contract development tools and programming languages (such as Solidity) to build and deploy smart contracts, while taking advantage of BSC's low costs and high throughput.

4. **Low transaction costs and high throughput**:
   - Binance Smart Chain offers relatively low transaction costs and fast transaction confirmation, allowing developers and users to use and interact with DApps at lower cost and greater efficiency.

5. **Cross-chain compatibility**:
   - Through the integration of Binance Chain and BSC, BSC has cross-chain compatibility, allowing assets to be transferred quickly and conveniently between Binance Chain and BSC, giving users broader use cases and flexibility.

### Use Cases and Ecosystem of Binance Smart Chain

- **Decentralized finance (DeFi)**:
  - BSC's DeFi ecosystem includes decentralized exchanges (DEX), lending platforms, stablecoins, and liquidity mining protocols, attracting large numbers of users and projects.

- **Gaming and digital art**:
  - BSC provides infrastructure for issuing and trading non-fungible tokens (NFT) and for high-speed game interaction, giving game developers and artists an innovative platform.

- **Cross-chain asset transfer**:
  - BSC's cross-chain capability lets users move assets quickly to Binance Chain or other supported blockchain networks, increasing asset liquidity and convenience of use.

### Development and Future Outlook of Binance Smart Chain

- Since its launch, Binance Smart Chain has developed rapidly; its low cost and high performance have attracted developers and users worldwide. As the ecosystem keeps expanding and technology advances, BSC is expected to keep playing an important role in DeFi, NFT, gaming, and other blockchain application areas, and to drive the adoption and application of blockchain technology.

Overall, through its innovative dual-chain architecture and efficient consensus mechanism, Binance Smart Chain provides developers and users with a powerful blockchain platform that promotes the development and adoption of decentralized applications, making it one of the key pieces of infrastructure for today's and tomorrow's digital economy.
