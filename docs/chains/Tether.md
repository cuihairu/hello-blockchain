# Tether

Tether (USDT) is not an independent blockchain project but a cryptocurrency built on blockchain technology, known as a stablecoin. A stablecoin's defining feature is a relatively stable price, usually pegged to a fiat currency (such as the US dollar) or another asset (such as gold) to reduce volatility. The following is a detailed introduction to the Tether project:

## Whitepaper and Specifications

No formal whitepaper — the [official transparency page](https://tether.to/en/transparency) and the reserve composition reports serve as the reference (the BDO attestation is an assurance engagement, not a full audit). USDT was issued on the Omni layer starting in 2015 and later expanded to multiple chains.

## What Problem It Solves

On-chain activity needs a unit of account anchored to fiat — trading, exchange, and settlement all need a base currency that "does not drift" (see the pricing role in the [settlement layer](/layers/Settlement)).

## Technical Achievements and Contributions

- **The largest stablecoin issuer**: USDT became the liquidity base layer across chains, spanning the Omni layer, Ethereum ERC-20, TRON TRC-20, and other standards.
- **Multi-chain issuance and redemption channels**: turned "issuer custody + multi-chain mapping" into an industry template.
- **Regular disclosure of reserve composition**: controversies aside, its disclosure frequency and detail rank high among comparable issuers.

## Limitations and Public Incidents

- Centralized custody and the power to freeze (law-enforcement freezes happen frequently); reserve transparency remains in long-running dispute.
- Public incidents: a bank-run rumor briefly dropped USDT to $0.90 in 2018-10; in 2021-02 the NYAG imposed an $18.5 million fine (misleading statements about reserves); in 2021-10 the CFTC imposed a $41 million fine (untrue statements about reserves).

## Supplementary Notes
### An Overview of Tether

1. **Types of stablecoins**:
   - Tether was first issued on the Bitcoin blockchain via the Omni Layer protocol, and later grew to support multiple blockchain networks such as Ethereum (the ERC-20 protocol), the TRON network (the TRC-20 protocol), and the Algorand network. Tether on each network is a distinct token, such as USDT (ERC-20) and USDT (TRC-20).

2. **The price stability mechanism**:
   - Tether's price stability rests mainly on the asset reserves behind it. According to Tether's official statements, every USDT token should be backed by assets equal in value to one US dollar (such as cash and cash equivalents), keeping the price stable.

3. **Circulation and applications**:
   - As a stablecoin, Tether is widely used across the cryptocurrency market for trading, payments, and fund transfers. Its stability has made it one of the primary trading pairs and stores of funds on cryptocurrency exchanges.

4. **Audits and transparency**:
   - Tether claims to undergo independent audits regularly, to verify that its holdings correspond to the USDT in circulation. Transparency and the audit process are critical for stablecoin users and market participants, underpinning trust in the stablecoin.

5. **Law and regulation**:
   - As one of the most popular stablecoins on the market, Tether faces scrutiny and legal constraints from regulators. The company maintains regulatory compliance measures across jurisdictions to meet local and international financial regulations.

### Tether's Applications and Impact

- **Trading and payments**: Tether is widely used for trading and payments in the cryptocurrency market, especially on exchanges without direct fiat support, serving as a stable trading pair and reserve of funds.

- **Financial services**: stablecoins offer convenient cross-border payment and settlement solutions worldwide, along with potential applications in financial services such as lending and asset management.

- **Market liquidity**: as one of the main liquidity providers in the cryptocurrency market, Tether has a significant impact on the stability and liquidity of the market as a whole.

All in all, Tether as a stablecoin plays an important role in the cryptocurrency field through its price stability and wide range of applications. However, given the concerns around the reserves behind it and its legal compliance, its future development and influence also face multiple challenges and uncertainties.
