# Bytom

Bytom is a blockchain platform focused on asset exchange and distributed registration, aimed at enabling circulation and interoperability between different assets. The following is a detailed introduction to the Bytom project:


## Whitepaper and Specifications

The Bytom whitepaper — Changjia team, 2017-06; mainnet launched in 2018-04; specifications follow the official GitHub ([github.com/Bytom](https://github.com/Bytom)).

## What Problem It Solves

How do "bit assets" (native digital assets) and "byte assets" (real-world assets brought on-chain) interact on the same chain — an early local exploration of asset swapping and registration.

## Technical Contributions

- **BUTXO + BVM**: general contract constraints attached to UTXO, balancing parallel verification with expressiveness; see [UTXO](/model/UTXO).
- **Three-layer architecture**: data operation layer / asset interaction layer / block layer kept separate.
- **Equity asset specification**: a contract template language aimed at asset registration.

## Additional Notes
### Overview of Bytom

1. **Background and goals**:
   - Bytom is a blockchain project developed by a Chinese team, founded in 2017. Its goal is to use blockchain technology to achieve secure management of digital assets, cross-chain trading, and smart contract functionality, aiming to provide a more convenient solution for the circulation of different assets.

2. **Blockchain technology**:
   - Bytom adopts a distinctive blockchain design based on the UTXO (Unspent Transaction Output) model, supporting storage and trading of multiple assets. The model is similar to the design of Bitcoin and Litecoin, but Bytom also introduces some custom features and protocols.

3. **Consensus algorithm**:
   - Bytom initially adopted a consensus algorithm called Tensority, and later moved to a hybrid consensus mechanism combining PoW (Proof of Work) and PoS (Proof of Stake). This hybrid consensus is intended to improve the network's security and efficiency.

4. **Cross-chain protocol**:
   - One of Bytom's core technologies is its Cross-chain Protocol, which allows digital assets between different blockchains to be exchanged securely and reliably. This provides the technical support for multi-chain interoperability, enabling assets to move freely across different blockchain networks.

5. **Smart contracts and application scenarios**:
   - Bytom supports the development of applications based on smart contracts; its smart contract platform can be extended to realize a variety of application scenarios, including decentralized finance (DeFi), asset management, and supply chain tracking.

6. **Development and community**:
   - The Bytom team is committed to promoting innovation and application in blockchain technology, actively participating in the development and standardization work of the blockchain industry. Its community is growing actively worldwide, supporting further expansion and optimization of the platform.

### Future Outlook for Bytom

As a blockchain project focused on asset exchange and multi-chain interoperability, Bytom is committed to expanding its influence in digital asset management and blockchain application development through technological innovation and application promotion. As blockchain technology develops and market demand changes, Bytom may continue to optimize its technical architecture and features to adapt to future challenges and opportunities.
